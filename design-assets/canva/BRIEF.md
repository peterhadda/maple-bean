## Deliverables

1. **Brand kit** (upload to Canva Brand Kit): colours above, Fraunces + Nunito, logo mark (Figma
   Components → Logo Mark), headshots of the four residents.
2. **Brand board** (1920×1080): palette, type specimen, `home-hero.png`, four headshots, taglines.
3. **Logo exploration board** (1920×1080), original directions only, no generic "cup with steam":
   leaf-bean (maple leaf whose vein is the bean crease) · open door (arched door, lamp glow) · two mugs forming a
   leaf in negative space · hanging blade sign · Bean Card roundel stamp.
4. **Social set**
   - 1080×1080 "Meet the regulars": one post per resident (`idle-34-front` + headshot + one-line personality).
   - 1080×1920 story "Your seat is saved": `study-nook.png` + "Study with me at Maple Bean".
   - 1080×1080 "Come as you are": `entrance.png`.
   - 1500×500 header: `hero-wide.png` crop + wordmark.
5. **Pitch / presentation deck** (16:9, ~8 slides): the third place idea → living café (`home-hero`) →
   residents (turnarounds) → study (`study-2-behind` vs `study-nook`) → mini-games (placeholder slide, prototypes
   in progress) → roadmap.

## Do not

- Copy Club Penguin art, logo, fonts, layouts, icons, colours or characters; no penguins, snow or igloos.
- Show Leo or Noah as final art (no game model yet); if needed, label "coming soon" without a figure.
- Crop residents at the ankles in full-body layouts; keep the same scale across a lineup.
