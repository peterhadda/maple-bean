# A2 · Environment gaps vs references (A2-01)

References: `design-assets/reference/cast-and-cafe.jpg` (top-right interior) and `design-assets/reference/floor-plan.webp`.
Compared against `qa/expansion/baseline/*.png` and `qa/expansion/environment/pass0/` (entrance→bar and evening views).
Ranked by visible impact (how much of the frame each gap occupies, in how many views).

1. **Lighting is flat and even; no warm pools, weak depth.** Hemisphere + environment fill light every wall the same; the 8 pendant point lights have no visible pools on floor/tables, and there is no darkening where walls meet the floor or ceiling. The *Cozy evening* preset looks almost identical inside (`pass0/12-evening-bar.png` vs `baseline/01-bar.png`) and the exterior walls stay bright at night (`pass0/13-evening-overview.png`). The reference has golden pendant pools, glowing bulbs, darker corners and sun shafts. Views: every interior view, especially `01-bar`, `02-lounge-fire`, `03-community-window`.
2. **The coffee bar is sparse and generic.** The espresso machine is a plain box, the pastry case holds 3 buns, the menu board has 3 lines, and the shelves hold identical red and white cylinders. The back wall is bare and there is no grinder, cup stacks, jars, register or greenery. The reference has a chrome two-group machine, grinders, a full tiered pastry case, a large chalk menu with side board, open shelves of jars, cups and plants, and a logo sign. Views: `01-bar`, `pass0/01b-entrance-to-bar`, `07-storefront` (through the door).
3. **The greenery is repetitive and not natural.** One rubber-plant species is repeated in every pot. Trees are clusters of spheres (`00-overview`, `11-plan`, and through the windows in `05-study-room`). There are no hanging or trailing plants, and the ceiling has no beams in walk mode. The reference has monstera, pothos trailing from shelves, snake plants, ferns, hanging planters from wooden beams and bushy layered trees. Views: `00-overview`, `11-plan`, `01b`, `03-community-window`, `06-games-garden`.
4. **The furniture is boxy.** 29 chairs are slab seats with a flat board back; sofas and armchairs are rectangular blocks. The reference has curved café chairs with rounded upholstered backs and slim legs, plump cushioned sofas and beanbags. Views: `02-lounge-fire`, `03-community-window`, `05-study-room`, `06-games-garden`.
5. **The windows and storefront are plain.** The windows are large empty openings with cream uprights. The reference has dark steel multi-pane windows. The street side has no benches, bikes, planters, A-frame sign or street lamp, and no lush trees along the facade. Views: `03-community-window`, `04-reading-books`, `07-storefront`, `00-overview`.

Also noted (lower impact): bare wall areas and empty floor between the bar and the entrance (`01b`); few small props such as books, cups, laptops, lamps, frames, cushions and rugs; no gaming corner with a TV or beanbags; no study booths.
